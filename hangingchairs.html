Oak with its history still visible

01 / Material provenance

This oak held wine for years.

Before it became a seat, this oak was part of a working wine barrel. Years of contact with wine leave their record in the wood: shifts in tone, deep grain and marks made in use.

We keep that record in view. A dark line or cooper's mark is not something to disguise; it is evidence of the timber's first life. No two pieces of oak carry the same history.

The material is not made to look old. It already is.

03 / A second life, made by hand

The barrel is the beginning.

The oak sets the terms. Its curve, colour and working marks inform how each piece is considered and made.

  1. 01

    Choose for character

    Reclaimed wine-barrel oak is assessed for the grain, markings and natural curve it brings to the design.

  2. 02

    Shape with restraint

    The timber is made into a seat while its former life remains legible in the finished piece.

  3. 03

    Make for use

    Each design is finished for its intended setting. Ask us about care, installation and the details of a particular piece.

Grain, colour and the traces of use are part of the design.

A note on hanging pieces

A swing is only as secure as its support.

Hanging furniture must be fixed to a structurally suitable support using appropriately rated fixings. Have the support and installation assessed by a qualified professional. Contact us for product-specific hanging guidance before installation.
